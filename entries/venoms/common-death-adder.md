## Overview

Death adders are short, stout ambush predators that may remain motionless and rely on camouflage, increasing the risk of accidental contact.

## Mechanism

Neurotoxins prevent normal nerve signals from activating muscles, including breathing muscles.

## Symptoms

Weakness can progress despite limited local injury. Early emergency care allows monitoring and respiratory support.

## Treatment

Do not disturb the bandage once applied. If breathing stops, begin CPR and follow emergency-dispatch instructions.

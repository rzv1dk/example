## Overview

The classic Sydney funnel-web is one of several medically important Australian funnel-web species. Any bite from a large black funnel-web-like spider is an emergency.

## Mechanism

Venom disrupts normal sodium-channel signalling and can rapidly affect the autonomic nervous system and breathing.

## Symptoms

Severe envenomation may progress quickly. Do not wait to identify the spider or for systemic symptoms to appear.

## Treatment

Pressure immobilisation is recommended for suspected funnel-web bites. Do not use it for redback bites.

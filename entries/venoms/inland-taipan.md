## Overview

The inland taipan is native to inland Australia and is rarely encountered by people. Any suspected bite must still be treated as an emergency.

## Mechanism

Multiple venom components can affect nerve transmission, blood clotting, and skeletal muscle.

## Symptoms

Early symptoms may be nonspecific before neurological or clotting effects become apparent.

## Treatment

Do not wash the bite site, cut the wound, or attempt suction. Keep the patient and bitten limb still while awaiting emergency care.

## Overview

The eastern brown snake is widespread in eastern Australia and may live near farms and urban edges where rodents are available.

## Mechanism

Its venom can rapidly disrupt coagulation and may also affect nerve and cardiovascular function.

## Symptoms

The bite can be painless and difficult to see. Do not wait for symptoms before seeking emergency help.

## Treatment

Do not wash, cut, or suck the bite. Do not use a tourniquet. Keep the person and limb still until paramedics arrive.

## Overview

Stonefish are well-camouflaged venomous fish found in northern Australian coastal waters. Stings usually occur when a person steps on or handles one.

## Mechanism

Pressure on dorsal spines injects heat-labile venom into tissue.

## Symptoms

Pain is typically immediate and disproportionate to the visible wound.

## Treatment

Hot-water immersion can reduce pain but must not be hot enough to burn. Hospital assessment is needed for severe pain or systemic symptoms.

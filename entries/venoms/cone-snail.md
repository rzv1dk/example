## Overview

Cone snails are predatory marine molluscs with a harpoon-like tooth used to inject venom. Live specimens should never be handled.

## Mechanism

Different conotoxins interrupt electrical and chemical signalling between nerves and muscles.

## Symptoms

Local effects can progress to widespread weakness and respiratory paralysis after a serious sting.

## Treatment

Urgent breathing support is the priority if paralysis develops. Do not cut, suck, or wash the puncture site when following pressure-immobilisation guidance.

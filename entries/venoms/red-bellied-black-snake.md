## Overview

The red-bellied black snake is associated with waterways and moist habitats in eastern Australia.

## Mechanism

Venom can affect local tissue, skeletal muscle, and blood clotting.

## Symptoms

Apparent species identification must never delay first aid or transport.

## Treatment

Treat any suspected snake bite as potentially life-threatening. Keep the patient at rest and await paramedics.

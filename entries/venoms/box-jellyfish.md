## Overview

The Australian box jellyfish is found in tropical coastal waters. Long tentacles carry millions of microscopic stinging cells.

## Mechanism

Contact triggers nematocysts to discharge venom through the skin. A large contact area can deliver a life-threatening dose rapidly.

## Symptoms

Pain and visible skin marks are typically immediate. Collapse is possible after extensive envenomation.

## Treatment

First-aid recommendations vary by species and region, so local emergency and beach-safety guidance should be followed immediately.

## Overview

The bluebottle is a floating siphonophore commonly washed onto Australian beaches. Detached tentacles can still sting.

## Mechanism

Microscopic nematocysts fire on contact and inject venom along the tentacle line.

## Symptoms

Most stings are intensely painful but limited to the skin. Call 000 for collapse or breathing difficulty.

## Treatment

For a confirmed bluebottle sting, Australian guidance recommends seawater removal and hot-water immersion; vinegar may worsen pain.

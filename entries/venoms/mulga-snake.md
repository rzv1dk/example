## Overview

Despite the alternative name “king brown,” the mulga snake belongs to the black-snake genus Pseudechis and occurs across much of inland Australia.

## Mechanism

Venom effects may include local injury, myotoxicity, and changes to haemostasis.

## Symptoms

Systemic symptoms and muscle damage can evolve after the initial bite.

## Treatment

All Australian snake bites should be treated as emergencies. Do not wash the bite site or use a tourniquet.

## Overview

Blue-ringed octopuses are small marine animals found in Australia and the western Pacific. Their bright blue rings become conspicuous when the animal is threatened.

## Mechanism

Tetrodotoxin prevents nerves from activating muscles. A conscious patient can become unable to move or breathe without losing awareness.

## Symptoms

Because the bite may be painless and subtle, any suspected contact followed by numbness or weakness is an emergency.

## Treatment

Rapid breathing support can keep the person alive while the toxin wears off and must continue until medical help takes over.

## Overview

Mouse spiders are burrowing Australian spiders in the genus Missulena. Clinically important bites are uncommon but must be treated cautiously.

## Mechanism

Venom composition varies by species; some toxins resemble those of funnel-web spiders.

## Symptoms

Because identification is difficult, first aid should be based on the possibility of serious envenomation.

## Treatment

Keep the patient still and leave the pressure bandage in place until emergency clinicians take over.

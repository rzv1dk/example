## Overview

“Irukandji” describes a group of small jellyfish capable of causing Irukandji syndrome, mainly in tropical Australian waters.

## Mechanism

The sting may initially seem minor before widespread pain and autonomic effects develop.

## Symptoms

Delayed severe pain, sweating, restlessness, and vomiting after a tropical-water sting require urgent assessment.

## Treatment

Vinegar first aid is recommended for suspected tropical stingers in Australia. Do not use fresh water to wash tentacles away.

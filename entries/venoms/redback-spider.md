## Overview

Redback spiders are found throughout Australia, often in dry, sheltered structures around homes and workplaces.

## Mechanism

Venom stimulates nerve terminals, producing the painful syndrome known as latrodectism.

## Symptoms

Pain and sweating are characteristic. Severity varies, and many bites do not cause systemic illness.

## Treatment

Use a cold pack wrapped in cloth. Pressure immobilisation can worsen pain and is not recommended for redback bites.

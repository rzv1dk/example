## Overview

The black mamba is among the fastest and most feared venomous snakes in sub-Saharan Africa. Its name comes from the ink-black interior of its mouth, not its skin color.

## Mechanism

Venom neurotoxins block signal transmission at the neuromuscular junction, producing rapid, progressive paralysis that can reach the respiratory muscles.

## Symptoms

Onset is fast relative to many other snake venoms — early neurological signs can appear within 15–30 minutes of a significant envenomation.

## Treatment

This is a medical emergency. Antivenom and respiratory support in a hospital setting are the standard of care; there is no effective first-aid substitute for professional treatment.

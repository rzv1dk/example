## Overview

The coastal taipan occurs in warm coastal regions of northern and eastern Australia and in New Guinea.

## Mechanism

Venom effects can progress rapidly, especially neurological weakness and consumption of clotting factors.

## Symptoms

Any suspected bite requires immediate emergency treatment, even if the person initially feels well.

## Treatment

Do not wash the bite site or try to capture the snake. Emergency clinicians use clinical findings and venom testing where appropriate.

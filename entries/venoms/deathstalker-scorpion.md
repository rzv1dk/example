## Overview

Despite the name, deathstalker stings are rarely fatal to healthy adults, though they are extremely painful. Risk is highest for young children, the elderly, and those with existing heart or respiratory conditions.

## Mechanism

Venom components interfere with normal ion channel function in nerve cells, which is what produces the disproportionate pain relative to the size of the animal.

## Symptoms

Local pain is usually severe and immediate; systemic symptoms, when they occur, tend to develop over the following hours.

## Treatment

Most healthy adults recover with supportive care and pain management alone. Seek medical attention promptly, especially for children.

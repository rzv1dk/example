## Overview

Tiger snakes occur in southern Australia, including wetlands and coastal environments. Colour and banding vary substantially.

## Mechanism

Several toxin families can affect nerve transmission, coagulation, and skeletal muscle.

## Symptoms

Local appearance does not reliably indicate the severity of envenomation.

## Treatment

Do not allow the person to walk. Leave the bandage in place for emergency clinicians to remove in a controlled setting.

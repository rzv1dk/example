## Overview

False morels can be confused with true morels. Preparation practices do not make an unidentified specimen reliably safe.

## Mechanism

The hydrazine metabolite interferes with GABA synthesis and cellular processes, explaining seizures and organ injury.

## Symptoms

The delay before illness can create false reassurance. Severe poisoning may include refractory seizures and liver failure.

## Treatment

Hospital clinicians may use targeted seizure treatment and monitor liver, kidney, blood, and metabolic function.

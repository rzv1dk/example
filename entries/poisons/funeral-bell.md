## Overview

Galerina marginata is a small wood-rotting mushroom containing the same amatoxin class responsible for death-cap poisoning.

## Mechanism

Amatoxins block cellular transcription. The liver receives a major absorbed dose and may fail after initial gastrointestinal symptoms subside.

## Symptoms

The symptom-free interval is dangerous and does not mean recovery.

## Treatment

Early hospital toxicology care is critical. Bring specimens or photographs, but never delay transport to collect them.

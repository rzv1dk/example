## Overview

The deadly webcap is an orellanine-containing Cortinarius associated with severe delayed kidney poisoning.

## Mechanism

Orellanine injury develops slowly, so the absence of early symptoms does not indicate safety.

## Symptoms

Kidney failure can first become apparent days after the mushroom meal, when preventive options are limited.

## Treatment

Early professional assessment is essential. Patients may need repeated kidney-function monitoring well after ingestion.

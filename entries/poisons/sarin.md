## Overview

Sarin is a highly toxic nerve agent. Exposure can occur through vapour or contaminated liquid.

## Mechanism

Acetylcholinesterase inhibition causes an uncontrolled cholinergic crisis affecting secretions, muscles, and breathing.

## Symptoms

Symptoms can progress rapidly. Multiple affected people or sudden unexplained secretions and pinpoint pupils are major warning signs.

## Treatment

Only trained responders should enter a contaminated area. Antidotes are most effective when given promptly by emergency teams.

## Overview

Earthballs are rounded fungi that may be mistaken for edible puffballs or underground fungi when immature.

## Mechanism

Ingestion most often produces gastrointestinal irritation; toxicity and identification can vary among species.

## Symptoms

Because a mixed foraged meal may contain several fungi, report everything eaten to the poison specialist.

## Treatment

Do not induce vomiting or try home remedies. Retain specimens and photographs if available.

## Overview

Chlorophyllum brunneum is one of several large parasol-like mushrooms associated with poisonings in Victoria.

## Mechanism

Toxicity is mainly gastrointestinal and individual susceptibility may vary.

## Symptoms

Symptoms beginning soon after a mushroom meal still require poison-centre advice because mixed collections may contain more dangerous species.

## Treatment

Keep any uncooked specimens for professional identification. Do not rely on internet photographs to declare a mushroom edible.

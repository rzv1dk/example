## Overview

Hydrogen fluoride is used in metal, glass, electronics, and chemical industries. In water it forms hydrofluoric acid.

## Mechanism

Unlike many acids, fluoride can penetrate deeply and disturb calcium-dependent body functions.

## Symptoms

Pain and visible injury may be delayed after dilute exposure, so apparently minor contact still requires urgent assessment.

## Treatment

Immediate water irrigation and emergency toxicology advice are essential. Do not delay while looking for specialised treatments.

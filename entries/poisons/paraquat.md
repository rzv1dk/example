## Overview

Paraquat is a highly toxic herbicide. Even small swallowed amounts can cause fatal systemic injury.

## Mechanism

Redox cycling generates damaging reactive oxygen species, with prominent effects in lung tissue.

## Symptoms

Early gastrointestinal injury can be followed by kidney, liver, and respiratory complications.

## Treatment

Do not induce vomiting unless directed by a poison centre. Rapid specialist advice is critical.

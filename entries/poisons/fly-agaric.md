## Overview

Fly agaric is the familiar red-and-white Amanita introduced to Australia and often associated with pine or birch trees.

## Mechanism

Its main neurotoxins are ibotenic acid and muscimol; despite the name history, its syndrome is not primarily caused by muscarine.

## Symptoms

Behaviour and consciousness can fluctuate between agitation and deep sedation.

## Treatment

Do not induce vomiting. Protect the person from injury and call 000 if they are difficult to wake, seizing, or having breathing problems.

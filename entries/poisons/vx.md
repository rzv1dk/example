## Overview

VX is a persistent, highly toxic nerve agent that can be absorbed through skin, eyes, lungs, or the gastrointestinal tract.

## Mechanism

Acetylcholinesterase inhibition creates a life-threatening cholinergic crisis.

## Symptoms

Very small skin exposures may first cause local sweating or twitching before systemic symptoms appear.

## Treatment

Do not approach contaminated material. Rapid decontamination, antidotes, and assisted ventilation may be lifesaving.

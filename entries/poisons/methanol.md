## Overview

Methanol, sometimes called wood alcohol, is used in industrial products and fuels. Poisoning often follows ingestion of contaminated alcohol or methanol-containing products.

## Mechanism

Its toxic metabolites cause acid to build up in the blood and can permanently damage vision and the nervous system.

## Symptoms

An initially well-appearing person can deteriorate after a delay, so suspected exposure requires assessment before symptoms emerge.

## Treatment

Early antidote treatment can slow formation of toxic metabolites. Severe cases may require haemodialysis.

## Overview

Arsine is a colourless, highly toxic gas most often associated with accidental industrial generation when arsenic-containing materials contact acid.

## Mechanism

The gas enters the bloodstream through the lungs and damages red blood cells.

## Symptoms

Early symptoms may be nonspecific. Dark urine or jaundice after a possible exposure is a medical emergency.

## Treatment

Hospital treatment focuses on supporting circulation, kidneys, and oxygen delivery.

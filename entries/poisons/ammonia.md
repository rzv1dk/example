## Overview

Ammonia is a colourless gas with a strong odour. It is used in refrigeration, agriculture, manufacturing, and some cleaning products.

## Mechanism

Contact with moisture produces a corrosive alkaline solution that damages tissue directly.

## Symptoms

Effects depend on concentration, route, and duration. Respiratory symptoms can worsen after exposure.

## Treatment

Do not enter a contaminated space without appropriate protection. Emergency teams can decontaminate and support breathing safely.

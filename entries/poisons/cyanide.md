## Overview

Cyanide describes several fast-acting chemicals found in some industrial processes and combustion products, as well as small amounts in certain natural materials.

## Mechanism

Although oxygen may be present in the blood, cyanide prevents cells from using it effectively, rapidly affecting organs with high energy needs.

## Symptoms

The presentation depends on the chemical form and route. A large exposure can cause collapse with little warning.

## Treatment

This is an immediate medical emergency. Do not induce vomiting after ingestion and do not enter a contaminated space without appropriate protection.

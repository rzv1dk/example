## Overview

Strychnine is a highly toxic alkaloid historically used in some pesticides and encountered in deliberate or accidental poisoning.

## Mechanism

Loss of normal inhibitory signalling produces severe, stimulus-sensitive muscle spasms while consciousness may initially be preserved.

## Symptoms

Repeated spasms can prevent breathing and cause dangerous metabolic complications.

## Treatment

Keep the environment quiet while awaiting emergency care. Hospital treatment prioritises airway control and rapid suppression of spasms.

## Overview

Phosgene is an industrial chemical and can also be generated when some chlorinated compounds are heated.

## Mechanism

Its direct chemical injury to the lung can continue after a person leaves the source.

## Symptoms

A deceptive period of improvement can occur before serious breathing problems develop.

## Treatment

Potentially significant exposure warrants emergency assessment and observation. Rescuers must not enter an unsafe atmosphere.

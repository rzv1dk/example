## Overview

The green-spored parasol is a common cause of mushroom gastroenteritis in regions where it occurs and can resemble edible parasol mushrooms.

## Mechanism

Its toxins primarily affect the gastrointestinal tract rather than producing the delayed liver syndrome of amatoxins.

## Symptoms

Rapid onset suggests an irritant syndrome, but mixed-species meals can make timing unreliable.

## Treatment

Medical treatment focuses on controlling vomiting and replacing fluid and electrolytes.

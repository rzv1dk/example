## Overview

Sulfur mustard is a blistering chemical warfare agent. The name “mustard gas” is historical; it may be present as a liquid or vapour.

## Mechanism

Cellular damage develops gradually, so serious injury can occur before pain or visible changes appear.

## Symptoms

The eyes and moist skin are especially sensitive. Severe exposure can injure airways and suppress bone marrow.

## Treatment

Early decontamination can reduce absorbed dose. Do not touch contaminated clothing or material directly.

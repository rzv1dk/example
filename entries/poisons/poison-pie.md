## Overview

Poison pie is one of the toxic wild mushrooms highlighted by SA Health. Common names are not reliable identifiers.

## Mechanism

Its principal clinical effect is an acute gastrointestinal syndrome.

## Symptoms

Early vomiting and diarrhoea may look like ordinary food poisoning, but professional advice is still necessary after any unknown mushroom ingestion.

## Treatment

Call promptly rather than waiting to see whether symptoms settle. Take a sample or photograph to hospital if requested.

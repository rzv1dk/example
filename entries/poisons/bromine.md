## Overview

Bromine is a reddish-brown liquid that readily produces irritating vapour. It is used in industrial and water-treatment applications.

## Mechanism

It reacts with water in tissues and causes direct chemical injury.

## Symptoms

Severity depends on dose and route. Significant inhalation can cause delayed respiratory deterioration.

## Treatment

Avoid secondary exposure. Decontamination and respiratory support should be directed by emergency professionals.

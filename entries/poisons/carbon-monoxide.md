## Overview

Carbon monoxide is a colourless, odourless gas produced by incomplete combustion. It can accumulate around faulty heaters, indoor generators, engines, fires, and other fuel-burning equipment.

## Mechanism

Carbon monoxide reduces the blood's ability to carry oxygen and also interferes with oxygen use inside cells.

## Symptoms

Early symptoms can resemble influenza or food poisoning. Multiple people becoming unwell in the same enclosed space is an important warning sign.

## Treatment

Do not enter an unsafe area to rescue someone. Emergency responders can control the source and provide oxygen safely.

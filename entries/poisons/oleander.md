## Overview

Oleander is a common ornamental shrub. All parts of the plant contain cardiac glycosides, and ingestion can cause serious poisoning.

## Mechanism

The glycosides alter ion transport in heart cells, which can trigger dangerous rhythm disturbances.

## Symptoms

Gastrointestinal symptoms may occur before or alongside cardiovascular effects. The severity cannot be judged safely at home.

## Treatment

Do not induce vomiting. Seek immediate expert advice after any suspected ingestion, especially involving a child or a deliberate exposure.

## Overview

Ghost fungus is a luminous Australian fungus that has been mistaken for edible oyster mushrooms.

## Mechanism

Ingestion causes an acute gastrointestinal irritant syndrome; glowing in the dark is not a safety indicator.

## Symptoms

Vomiting and diarrhoea may be intense enough to require medical rehydration.

## Treatment

Do not induce vomiting unless directed by a poison specialist. A photograph of the intact fungus and its growing location may assist identification.

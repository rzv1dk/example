## Overview

Chlorine is a yellow-green gas used in industry and water treatment. Mixing household bleach with incompatible cleaners can release toxic gas.

## Mechanism

Chlorine damages moist tissue by producing corrosive acids and reactive compounds.

## Symptoms

People with asthma or other lung disease may be especially sensitive. Do not rely on smell to judge safety.

## Treatment

Do not mix household cleaning products. Follow emergency instructions about evacuation or sheltering in place.

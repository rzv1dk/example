## Overview

The death cap is responsible for the majority of fatal mushroom poisonings worldwide. It closely resembles several edible species, making misidentification a leading cause of exposure.

## Mechanism

Amatoxins are absorbed slowly and concentrate in the liver, where they shut down the cell's ability to produce proteins — this is why symptoms are delayed and damage can be severe by the time it's diagnosed.

## Symptoms

The delayed onset and temporary apparent recovery are especially dangerous, as they can lead people to believe they are past the danger point when organ damage is still progressing.

## Treatment

Any suspected death cap ingestion is a medical emergency regardless of how the person currently feels — treatment outcomes are strongly tied to how early care begins.

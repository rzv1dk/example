## Overview

The yellow-staining mushroom is a frequent cause of wild-mushroom poisoning in Victoria and can be confused with edible Agaricus species.

## Mechanism

The exact irritant mixture is not fully characterised, but ingestion can produce substantial gastrointestinal illness.

## Symptoms

Severity varies with the amount eaten. Children and people unable to keep fluids down are at increased risk of dehydration.

## Treatment

Do not wait for symptoms before calling the Poisons Information Centre. Keep a sample and photographs for expert identification if this can be done safely.

## Overview

Sodium azide is used in laboratories and some industrial processes. Contact with acid can release toxic hydrazoic acid gas.

## Mechanism

It interferes with cellular respiration and vascular control, producing rapid systemic toxicity.

## Symptoms

Significant exposure may cause sudden cardiovascular and neurological deterioration.

## Treatment

Do not handle contaminated material without appropriate protection. Emergency observation is needed after suspected significant exposure.
